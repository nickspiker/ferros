You may need to confirm your physical location and call-back number.

      • (iv) emergency calls made while roaming may be routed to the local PSAP associated with your registered address;

      • (v) if you are deaf, hard of hearing, or speech impaired you should call local emergency services directly using TTY or a telecommunications relay service, instead of 711 or a local equivalent;
