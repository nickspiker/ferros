- **The "post-crash trap" was AVB, not a crash state (found 2026-11-06 night, cost the evening)**: akita's boot_b ends in Google's AVB footer, putting its vbmeta at G#FD_C000. A bare image larger than that (the 20 MB hv gate) buries the vbmeta; ABL then logs `boot_b: Error verifying vbmeta image: invalid vbmeta header` and drops to fastboot ("boot failure") without running anything, and every smaller image flashed after it fails the same way until Google's whole boot_b goes back (which is why "booting Android once" seemed to cure it). Every "ferros dies 1 s in" probe that night was this; ferros never ran. Fix (commit 98fd338): `ferros-mkimg avb-footer` (whole partition, own footer + vbmeta signed with keys/avb.vsf; unlocked ABL accepts the unchained key and loads exactly the image length). **Always flash akita's boot partitions with a footer.** Before blaming a boot, read ABL's log: "invalid vbmeta header" = AVB, "BL1 requested" = a stamped fastboot (a probe, ferros's own exit), the DSS "PRE" block = the previous session's crash, not this one.
