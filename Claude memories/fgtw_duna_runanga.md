The owner, 2026-11-05: FGTW is "a rural utility that nobody owns": a DUNA (Wyoming Decentralized Unincorporated Nonprofit Association). Anyone who buys service from FGTW is a member of the assembly, the governing body, which the owner calls the **rūnanga** (te reo Māori: council or assembly).

**Why:** FGTW is shared infrastructure (Photon bootstrap, fgtw.org). The model is a member-owned utility like a rural electric co-op, but with no owner at all.

**How to apply:**
- When governance, billing or membership comes up, members are service buyers, and the rūnanga is their assembly.
- Open points raised (not decided):
  - The member unit: per paid account, or per TOKEN identity with service (one person, one vote, Sybil-resistant through TOKEN).
  - The DUNA's 100-member floor.
  - The nonprofit-purpose limit: no distributions to members.
  - Tax status is separate from DUNA status.
  - How a worldwide assembly votes (quorum, notice, proxies), written into the governing principles.
- Counsel's ground, beside the counsel gate in docs/RELEASE.md.
