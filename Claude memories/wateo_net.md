**Wāteo** (wah-TAY-oh), wateo.net, bought 2026-11-09: data-only service at cost, run by its members, tied to the handle not the SIM, sponsor carriers the person picks. See [[mvno-data-only]] and [[fgtw-duna-runanga]].

- **Site:** static HTML + one style.css in `/mnt/Chiton/MEGA/wateo/site` (beside passless, not in git); only `site/` is published, so hui notes (`hui/`), deploy.sh and wrangler.toml stay off the web (2026-11-17: the whole folder had been uploaded, deploy.sh and wrangler.toml were public). Deploy with `./deploy.sh` (Pages project `wateo`, branch main). Pages (rebuilt 2026-11-17, owner: "That site was a placeholder so whatever you think"): Home, Why, How it works, Privacy, Principles, Requirements, Road, Books, Join, Limits, Contact. Audience: anyone, light data users first (per gigabyte), and people who hate telemarketing (no number; contact is mutual consent). Full disclosure: everything is public and linked (owner: "all of this will be public knowledge and most will be given away, aside from possibly spirix and PIPE"). No scripts. `_headers` sends `Cache-Control: no-transform` on every page, which stops Cloudflare injecting its email decoder, beacons or Rocket Loader with no dashboard toggle (verified 2026-11-09: the email obfuscation was injecting into Contact until the header went in). NEL/Report-To still come from the zone (passless strips them in a Function; wateo has none).
- **DNS:** zone in the owner's Cloudflare account (registrar Cloudflare); wateo.net CNAME → wateo.pages.dev proxied; mail is a Proton custom domain (MX, SPF, 3 DKIM CNAMEs DNS-only, DMARC quarantine); kaitiaki@wateo.net is the contact. Wrangler's OAuth can't edit DNS or zone settings; the owner does those in the dashboard. Only .net: no wateo.org/.com.
- **No dollars** (owner, 2026-11-09): dollars can't be governed in a decentralized way. Payment is crypto; fiat holders swap thru an onboarding provider such as Coinbase. Never write prices or flows in dollars. Why (owner, 2026-11-19): a dollar is under strict human control, which the owner is not against, but it means a government can make an account of any size disappear with a snap; a program-governed chain like Solana can't. So no dollar-pegged stablecoins either (USDC and USDT can be frozen by their issuers): native, program-governed coin only. Dollars appear only where the billing entity pays a fiat supplier (Hologram), never as what members pay or what prices are in.
- **The association is forming, not formed** (owner, 2026-11-09): 100 people to form the DUNA, at least 400 more to launch, likely no launch before ~1,000, invite only for now. Site footer says "(forming)".
- Principles is written (in site/; deployed only when the owner says) as "Version 0 · draft · not yet signed", drafted from fgtw RUNWAY.md (the 42-year plan: "year 4 of 42") with a seventh invariant added: nobody can connect a line to a person.
- **Unlinkability is the point** (owner, 2026-11-17: "there should be no way for me to connect the alias usage and billing with the customer, that's the whole point"). The site's Privacy page makes it the requirement: blind-signed credit (pay from any wallet, credit unlinkable to the line), a blind patron credential for membership, blind ballots, a Wāteo-only device key at the gateway. WATEO.md carries it since 2026-11-17: line and member never touch, every crossing blind (credit notes, served notes, invites, ballots), a member key beside the Wāteo key, a Wāteo device key over ira ‖ Wāteo key at the gateway, no binding for the Wāteo key (a binding names the handle proof), locate debited to the Wāteo key; W8 is the blind scheme.
- **Images** (2026-11-21): page pictures are AVIF (AV1 by rav1e: ffmpeg librav1e, or `cavif` for transparency since ffmpeg's rav1e has no alpha plane) in `site/img/`; the home logo has the kererū hero (`img/hero.avif`) perched on it, drawn at the disc's width (220 of logo.svg's 240 units, 11/12 of the logo) with its bottom edge at the disc's centre; the image itself must hold to that (width = the cut circle's diameter, bottom = its centre): the owner's 2026-11-21 cut ran about 3% large, fitted and padded to 590x827. Link previews are WebP, 1200x630, one per page in `site/og/` (owner's choice 2026-11-21: AVIF isn't read by Facebook, X and several messengers; previews only). Their art is a series of its own, never on the pages: a kererū (NZ wood pigeon, the "carrier" pigeon that doesn't know who you talk to) in a deadpan scene per page. Sources from Gemini sit in `bard images/`, outside site/.
- **Logo** (owner, 2026-11-09): `logo.svg` in the site folder: a solid disc in VSF RGB green brought to the sRGB gamut edge along the line from 523 nm to Illuminant E (VSF linear (0.406, 1, 0.406) = #00FF93; never the clipped #00FF00), "wāteo" in Comfortaa Bold outlined in black, the ā's macron and a bar under the word in E white #FFE5E1 (brightest Illuminant E in sRGB, never D65 #FFFFFF), text #000000. `favicon.svg` = disc + bar only. Built with inkscape (text-to-path, path-split for the macron).
- **Site theme "bands"** (owner, 2026-11-09): dark only, never dark text on a light page. Background the logo green at 1/64 linear (#00220F), nav band and doors at 1/32 (#003118), lines 1/16 (#004725), headings E white #FFE5E1, body half E half green (#90BC93), accent #00FF93, warn = vsf Orange to the gamut edge (#FF8B00). Nav is a full-width band, its Home link the favicon mark; Home shows logo.svg. No animations, no transitions, no pixel units, no breakpoint media queries: sizes in --ru only (the one aspect-ratio query defines --ru, as on ferros.org/passless.org).
- **On ferros: docs/WATEO.md is the destination** (owner, 2026-11-13: data ferros-only, paid by the identity; "make sure we have the destination defined so we don't ... need to redesign it again"): a Wāteo profile reaches only a private APN to Wāteo's gateway; data flows only in a tunnel opened with the kernel's person-key-derived Wāteo key + the device's ira key, so Android or a moved SIM get nothing; profiles are a pool's, service is the person's; a narrow device tunnel for locate while locked. Check its "Rules for what is built before real service" before touching the modem, `ip` routing or eSIM. Open: W0 tunnel protocol, W1 one device or several, W2 who runs the gateway, W3 bring-your-own SIM beside it.
- **Carrier access, Hologram** (owner, 2026-11-10): a Hologram IoT account with the organization "FGTW wateo"; its dashboard can test and add eSIM profiles on Verizon, AT&T and T-Mobile. The first step toward network switching. The eSIM SGP.22 client is the one place web encodings are allowed ferros-side ([[feedback-no-web-encodings]]); nothing of the account (logins, ICCIDs, IMEIs) goes in the repo.
