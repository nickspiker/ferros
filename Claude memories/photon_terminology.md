Owner (2026-10-15): "We call messages in Photon photons, we also call video calls beams."
- **photon** = a message → "send a photon".
- **beam** = a video call → "beam someone". The original overnight goal ("beam someone tomorrow with the full network and camera stack") meant a video call — hence camera.

**How to apply:** say photon/beam in conversation, docs and commit messages. Milestones on ferros: send a photon first (network + photon-core), then a beam (camera, video, audio on top). Related: [[photon_on_ferros_no_bootstrap]].
