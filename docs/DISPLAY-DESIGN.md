# DISPLAY-DESIGN — the husky display driver: decisions, plan, profiling, HDR

Decided with the owner 2026-10-16: **display first, prim and proper — every mode, 8 and 10 bit, all profiled — then the display pipe (the DPU), then the camera.** No driver code until this plan is agreed. DISPLAY.md is today's state (a frame pusher on the surface ABL leaves running); DISPLAY-HK3.md is the panel inventory; `oracle/display/husky-hk3/` is how Android drives it, captured live.

## 0. The panel is google-hk3

The first inventory here came from `panel-samsung-s6e3hc3-c10.c`, a sibling device's panel, which is why its geometry didn't match. The phone says `panel_name = google-hk3`, model HK3-A0B-00, on **DSIM0** driven by **DECON0**. Native 1344×2992 at 60/120 Hz; a 1008×2244 pair the **panel itself scales** (`C3 0D` + `F2 81`); LP (AOD) at 30 Hz in both sizes; idle floors of 1/10/30 Hz by the panel's auto-rate engine; a 60 Hz "normal speed" op mode (`60 18`) beside the 120 Hz "high speed" one. DSC 8 bpc / 8 bpp, two slices of 672×187. Brightness 2–1000 nits normal (DBV 196–3307), 1000–1600 nits HBM (3308–4095), default 140 nits. 4 DSI lanes at 1346 Mbps as Android runs them (1368 as ABL leaves them; ferros does either), command mode, TE on gpp0-3. Every byte of every transition: DISPLAY-HK3.md §3–5 and the oracle.

## 1. Inventory and oracle

- **DISPLAY-HK3.md**: modes, DSC/PPS fields, every command set and transition, brightness/HBM/ACL/dimming, TE/TE2, rails and reset, DSIM0/DPHY/DECON0 addresses, the stock driver's control surface — with line citations.
- **oracle/display/husky-hk3/**: for 26 states (each mode with idle off and on, idle floors, NS/HS op mode, brightness levels, HBM, dimming, AOD in/out, full OFF and ON): the DSI packets Android sent to get there (bytes, types, waits, timings), the DPU register dump afterwards (DECON0, windows, DPP, DMA, HDR, SRAMC, DSIM, DPHY, link), and the panel's sysfs. The `ON` capture is the complete power-on: PPS, `11`, the init set with its 10 ms and 110 ms waits, C3/F2, features, `29`. The DPU register dumps are the DECON/DSIM configuration for each mode, which no driver source gives directly.

## 2. Decisions (the owner's)

1. **All modes.** WQHD and FHD at 60 and 120, the panel's idle/auto floors (1/10/30), NS 60 Hz, AOD 30 Hz, HBM, dimming — each brought up and checked against the oracle's register state.
2. **8 and 10 bit, both, profiled.** Everything Android runs is 8 bpc DSC. The 10-bit path exists in DECON (`TEN_BPC_MODE`, 1010102 formats, dither/DQE) and the driver's F2 register is commented "8/10bit config"; whether the panel takes a 10 bpc PPS is UNCONFIRMED and is an experiment, not an assumption.
3. **"GPU" means the DPU** (DECON/DPP: scaling, blending, DQE, the 2D pipe to the panel). fluor is a CPU compositor; the shell never needs Mali, and a bare-metal Mali is a separate project.
4. **Cold power-up from off**: yes, prim and proper — rails, reset, the full init — not a dependence on ABL leaving the panel lit.
5. **The kernel keeps its pre-process painter** (Ready? gate, checklist); the display server takes over at first process.
6. **Camera after the display.** AoC audio after that.

## 3. Architecture

A **display server process** (EL0, masked/sealed) holding grants for DECON0, the DPP layers it uses, DSIM0 + DPHY, the DPU power/clock registers and the PMIC path for the panel rails; a DMA framebuffer inside its arc (non-cacheable, the S2MPU fence over it); and a `display` endpoint other processes draw through. The shell (SHELL.md) is a client of it. Why a process: the panel bring-up is long and full of waits and DCS round trips, a bad DCS write ends the server rather than the kernel, and the capability model already fits.

## 4. The plan, in order

1. **DSI command path** — DONE 2026-10-16 (DISPLAY.md): DCS writes and reads, brightness proven by readback, refresh 60 ⇄ 120 by the panel's own command sets (`hk3.rs`, `bridge display mode`), measured on the TE pin; DSIM's command-TE timers set per rate. Originally: Send DCS over DSIM0's FIFOs; prove it with the smallest visible commands and their oracle bytes: brightness `51 hh ll`, dimming `53 28/20`, display off/on `28` / `29` with the F4 Vreg/AMP bracket, HBM `53 E0` + `51 0F FF`. Then the mode switches: `60 01` / `60 00` with the `BD` early-exit table and the TE2 update, exactly as `W60_noidle` / `W120_noidle` show. This alone unblocks the wake policy and power saving.
2. **Our own framebuffer, and DECON configured like Android's.** Measured 2026-10-16 (`oracle/display/husky-hk3/tools`-style diff of the live registers against the W120 dump): ABL leaves DECON within 31 of 708 words of Android's 120 Hz state. What Android sets that ABL doesn't, which this step must set: `GLOBAL_CON.TEN_BPC_MODE` (DECON composes in **10-bit**), `DATA_PATH_CON_0` enhance path = dither + DQE (ABL: none), SRAM allocation for every window it uses (`SRAM_EN_OF_PRI_0/1`), out-FIFO QoS (`OF_URGENT_EN`, `OF_RD_URGENT_0/1`, `OF_DTA_CONTROL/THRESHOLD`), the interrupt enables, and per layer the HDR block (`HDR_LSI`: EOTF LUT, gain, OETF LUT — Android degammas each layer to linear, blends at 10 bpc, re-encodes) plus `DPP_COM` and `SRAMC` mode bits. So Android's "10-bit" is internal precision for colour management in front of an 8 bpc DSC link — exactly the role §6 gives it. Tried 2026-10-16: setting `TEN_BPC_MODE` with the dither bit but without the DQE's dither configuration and Android's DSC_CONTROL1 (G#55) stalls DECON on a frame timeout and nothing short of a reboot recovers it; the SRAM/QoS/interrupt part is in (`display tune`), the 10 bpc part turned out to need only the DQE in the enhance path and sized (its dither is one enable bit; the dump had the sizes) — done 2026-10-16 as a modeset option (`dpu.rs`, DISPLAY.md). The framebuffer itself — DONE 2026-10-16 as a region (`cachemap::DISPLAY_FB`, 32 MiB at G#8400_0000, non-cacheable, EL0 RW, DMA grant `display-fb`; `bridge display fb own`): the base layer reads it with no DMA error, so the DPU reaches ferros's DRAM unfenced. Left for the server: owning the layer registers itself (DECON0 window 5 / DPP0 / DMA0 from the dumps) instead of inheriting ABL's, and the shadow-update → trigger → frame-done loop off TE.
3. **Every mode** — DONE 2026-10-16, 10 bpc compose included (DISPLAY.md): FHD ⇄ WQHD by the full modeset (DISPLAY-MODESET.md), the auto-rate idle floors (30/10/1 Hz, measured with changeable TE), NS 60 (TE 1201 µs), AOD (30 Hz, 687 µs, DBV 853) and back, HBM and dimming. Each state's registers diffed against the oracle's dump: with `modeset … 10` the 10 bpc + dither + DQE words match too; what still differs is the window/buffer choices (ours), Android's AFBC bit and its DPP IRQ enable. The DSIM link matches word for word once the modeset takes Android's 1346 Mbps link rate (DISPLAY-HK3.md §7); at ABL's 1368 the PLL, lane timing and TE-timer words are the rate's own.
4. **Cold power-up.** VDDI → VCI → reset {1,1,5} ms → extinfo/ID reads → PPS → init (10 ms, 110 ms) → VDDD, per DISPLAY-HK3.md §6 — needs the S2MPG14/15 regulator path (LDO14, LDO22, BUCKA) from EL2; then the full `OFF` sequence (`28`, F4 writes, `10` + 100 ms, rails down). Verified by cutting the panel off and bringing it back without ABL.
5. **10-bit experiment.** DECON `TEN_BPC_MODE` + a 10 bpc PPS, with the F2 register's bits explored; the result is either a working 10-bit mode or a documented refusal. Dither through DECON either way (it is what makes 8-bit-under-HDR-headroom clean). **Prepared 2026-10-16** (`modeset … link10 [f2=XX]`, DISPLAY.md): the pipe runs it; the verdict needs eyes on the panel. The 10 bpc blend + DQE dither on the 8 bpc link is done and the default.
6. **The display server and `display` endpoint**, fluor's no_std path rendering the shell on the phone. **Running since 2026-10-16 night** (`payloads/display`, DISPLAY.md): the process owns the framebuffer and the layer, paints the first-boot screen with fluor + Oxanium, presents on TE, serves the `display` endpoint (begin / text / rect / present / info, plus brightness / refresh / idle floor / DCS through its own DSIM path, `ferros_dsi`), and asks the panel for the 10 Hz auto-rate floor between frames. `payloads/shelltest` draws SHELL.md's deck through it. Next: the shell as a process with live rows (Photon's conversations, the vault's sealed list), TE-paced presents with damage, AOD as a server state, the kernel's bench ops retired once nothing needs them.

## 5. Profiling: what "profiled" means here

- **Timing**: per mode, TE period and width (the panel's TE constants: 273 µs at 120, 8500 µs at 60 HS, 1223 at 60 NS, 693 in AOD), frame transfer time against the underrun budget, mode-switch latency (the oracle has the transition timings: a W120→W60 switch is 23 packets in ~6 ms plus the vsync waits).
- **Power**: per mode and brightness, measured — not estimated. **Done under ferros 2026-10-16: DISPLAY-POWER.md** (the gauge read over hsi2c_11 with the charger's buck off so the battery carries the system; solid-fill scenes; every mode, 8/10 bpc, both link rates, three brightnesses, HBM). Headline: content (+2.4 W white over black at 140 nits) and brightness (black alone moves 0.8 W between 2 and 140 nits) dwarf refresh rate (100–200 mW) and everything in the pipe (10 bpc, link rate, FHD: nothing measurable). Still to do: AOD, the Android baseline for the same scenes, 20 s averages.
- **Colour** — MEASURED 2026-10-17 with the owner's i1 Pro (DISPLAY-CALIB.md): native γ 2.185 with no toe, white 6750 K (not E), primaries wider than P3, DBV table within 5 %; the VSF→panel transfer table and the 3×3 follow from it.
- **10 bit**: whether it exists on the link (plan step 5), and if so, measured banding versus 8 bit + dither.

## 6. Rendering: gamma2tanh to the panel — decided 2026-10-17

Content arrives as VSF `gamma2tanh` (vsf colour.md: `v = tanh(√L)` per channel, white at code 194/256, 3.3 stops above white at 8 bits, 5.1 at 16). The display curve was chosen on the glass with the owner (the display server's test card, `shelltest 02`, against a code-255 peak line), the panel numbers are DISPLAY-CALIB.md. The order, per pixel, per channel:

1. **Decode once**: `L = atanh(v)²` — scene light in VSF RGB, white = 1. The encoding's tanh is storage; it is never re-applied.
2. **Matrix**: 3×3 VSF RGB → panel RGB in linear light, the white adaptation inside it: VSF's Illuminant E renders as the panel's native white (6750 K measured) at full luminance — display white = media white, the owner's choice (a). Negatives clamp to 0. The matrix comes from the panel primaries placed under the observer VSF uses (Stockman–Sharpe 10°, from the i1 Pro's saved spectra), not through 1931 xy.
3. **Expose**: one scalar — white's share of the panel's peak, 1/H (the brightness setting), times any per-image exposure. Linear, so it commutes with the matrix.
4. **Roll off, in gamma-2 space** (chosen on the glass 2026-10-17, "may change later"): `Y/peak = tanh²(√(L/H))` — the encoding's own order, tanh after the square root, applied with the display's headroom. Slope 1 at black, the bend begins around a quarter of white, smooth everywhere, never clips (96 % of peak at the encoding's top). White lands at tanh²(1/√H) of the peak: 21 % at H = 4, 41 % at H = 1.75, 58 % at H = 1 — so for a given white in nits the panel's peak is set 1/tanh²(1/√H) above it (2.46× at H = 1.75), which costs reach (605 nits of white under HBM instead of 767) and nothing in power for the same picture, since OLED pays per lit pixel. Compared at equal white against the linear-domain `tanh(L/H)`: that one holds a straight 0.79× slope below white and bends only near the top; the sqrt one keeps slope 1 in the shadows and spends more codes on the top stops — the owner read it as the smoother picture. A piecewise knee and the raw `tanh(√L)²` display curve were tried and rejected.
5. **Panel encode**: `code = Y^(1/2.185)` — the measured EOTF (a plain power law, no toe) — at 10 bits, then the DQE's dither to the 8 bpc DSC link.
6. **Brightness**: the setting is diffuse white in nits (the measured DBV table, 1353 = 133 nits on this unit), the panel runs at the DBV for the peak the headroom needs, HBM only when ambient asks (its timer makes H a function of time). OLED makes headroom cheap: only the highlight pixels pay.

**Where it runs: the DQE.** Steps 1, 2 and 4+5 are the DQE's three blocks — a **degamma LUT** (33 points, 13-bit, with programmable x positions, so the steep top third-stop of `atanh²` gets its own points), a **3×3 gamma matrix** (14-bit coefficients and offsets), and a **regamma LUT** (33 points per channel, programmable x — on a square-root grid, since the curve is steep near black) carrying `tanh²(√(L/H))` and the 2.185 encode — at 10 bits on every pixel, in hardware (`dqe_reg.c`: set_degamma_lut / set_gamma_matrix / set_regamma_lut; on zuma at G#1800 / G#1400 / G#2400, not regs-dqe.h's bare G#1000 / G#0C00 / G#2000: §6b). The compositor writes gamma2tanh codes straight into the framebuffer and never touches a pixel for colour; a brightness change is a new regamma table and a DQE shadow update. The display server owns the tables. Untagged (plain VSF RGB) content is the same path with nothing above white.

**Precision**: scaling white down to 1/H costs codes (at H = 4 white sits at 10-bit code 346); the 10 bpc blend and the dither are what keep 8-bit content clean there — the reason 10 bpc is the default even though the link is 8 (DISPLAY.md).

**Pinned (owner, 2026-10-17):** this pipeline is the reference colour profile for everything the owner's projects show or store. Mahere today stores imagery as √(tanh(x)) (tanh in linear light, then gamma 2) and displays through Opsin's cubic rail — a sibling; once the panel pipeline is final it ports to Mahere's imagery and to VSF's gamma2tanh definition, and the two are not allowed to drift meanwhile.

## 6a. As built (2026-10-24)

§6 was the plan; this is what runs. Two decisions moved since: **white is equal-energy E** (owner, 2026-10-22; not the panel's native 6750 K), and **the transform runs in software, in NEON, not in the DQE** — the DQE is in the path only for its dither (shift mode; the CGC dither at G#2800 on zuma, §6b) and the 10 bpc blend; its matrix and LUTs and the CGC are off. Software keeps the roll-off, the gamut handling and the dither's precision in our hands, and measured it is cheap enough (below).

**Content** (all VSF RGB, gamma 2 — the code is √light):
- the 8-bit canvas the display server composites (white = 255, nothing above);
- an app's 16-bit surface (white = Q14, to 4.0 = 16× white in light; bit 48 = no dither);
- icons: sRGB assets through vsf's SRGB2VSF_RGB colorimetrically (no adaptation), stored as gamma2tanh codes (`tools/app-icons`), decoded to the canvas's gamma 2;
- the map's own colours (greys, white, amber) authored as VSF RGB gamma-2 codes.
Two things skip the pipe on purpose: the ramps test screen (op 21, straight through the tone table) and the kernel's own screens before the server starts (8-bit, panel-native).

**Compositing** (payloads/display `draw.rs`, fluor): front to back, premultiplied, `under` — blending in gamma 2, as decided for paint. Circles are cached as sprites per size and turn (`display-sprites`, 32 MiB) and blitted four pixels a step. The glow is the exception that proves the rule only half way: it is added in gamma 2 too (the linear-light glow was tried and looked worse, 2026-10-24), falling off in log distance — Mitchell's log2 by `clz`, the same step every octave of q = d² − r², zero at r/12 past the edge at every size, no divide, no table.

**Since 2026-11-12 the transform is baked** (COMPOSITOR.md "Baked"; owner: "a straight thru render and a sqrt to get the gamma close"): steps 1-3 below run where a colour or an image is made (the palette once per colour, the icons and the wallpaper once per image, apps in their own process or on the GPU), the canvas holds the panel's codes, and the present is step 4 alone, with a 16 × 16 blue-noise dither in place of the Bayer. Steps 1-3 as they were:

**The present stage** (payloads/display `neon.rs` `Odt`, one branchless path for every pixel, 16 at a time):
1. light — canvas: u = c²/65536 (an exact u8 multiply, one fixed-point convert) into a degree-4 polynomial fitted to the roll-off `tanh²(k·√L)`, k = atanh(√(white/peak)), at each brightness; app: tanh by Lambert's continued fraction (7th order, f32), squared;
2. the ODT (DISPLAY-CALIB.md "The ODT"), gains + K in f16 — the gains are E's white, K the red-green correction;
3. the panel's 1/2.19 from the f16's bits (exponent → the octave's foot, top six mantissa bits → the nudge, interpolated); the tables are the gamut clamp (negatives to 0, past the peak saturates);
4. dither: Bayer 4×4 plus a temporal rank over four phase frames (flipped at 10 Hz at rest, consecutive phases under a finger) to the 8-bit code the link takes, 14 bits effective; packed BGRA1010102 by one `st4`.
Sixteen black pixels skip all of it.

**Frames**: the server replies to a frame before drawing it and keeps only the newest; op 24 (`frame wait`) returns when the panel's next frame starts, and the map waits on it once a pass with a finger down, so updates lock to the 120 Hz refresh (one a refresh while an update fits, a steady every-other when not). A tall publish is cut into row bands weighted by the cores' measured rates (X3 145/256, each A715 37/256).

**Measured** (PMU on the cores, `ferros_sys::pmu`; synthetic pans, `FERROS_MAP_START=drag|crowd`): the ODT 86 cycles per 16 pixels on the X3 (1.9 ns a pixel), a full-frame publish 5.2 ms banded; the map's seven circles 120 frames for 120 updates at ~3.7 ms an update; a 207-circle crowd 174 frames for 175 updates (scene 3.2 ms — glow 1.8, blits 1.2 — publish 3.0). Processes build without strict-align (`cargo el0`), which is what lets NEON loads and stores be single instructions.

**Tunables** (payloads/display): `GLOW_FRACTION` (12), `GLOW_OCT_LOG2` (2: four octaves), `GLOW_PEAK`, `PUB_SHARE`, `PUB_SPLIT_ROWS`; the ODT and E's gains come from `tools/panel-white-e.py`.

## 6b. The DQE on zuma: its map, what it can do, and whether it could carry the bake (2026-11-12)

Read from the stock driver (display-gs `cal_9865/regs-dqe.h` and `dqe_reg.c`, `cal_common/dqe_cal.h`, `exynos_drm_dqe.c`) and from the Pro's live registers after a jump (`bridge read`, read only), nothing written.

**The map.** regs-dqe.h gives each block's offset as the oldest DQE had it, and the driver adds an offset per version (dqe_cal.h `regs_dqe_offset`; zuma is DQE_V4): the two dithers and the regamma blocks +G#400, the two matrices and degamma +G#800, ATC and HSC as written, the histogram +G#400 and reached only by SMC (secure on zuma: never touched). ferros used the bare offsets until 2026-11-12. What it set as the display dither (G#2800) is the CGC dither, what it cleared as the CGC dither (G#2400) is regamma 0's enable, and the never-called `dqe_quiet` cleared words that are no stage at all (G#0C00, G#1000, G#2000, G#4800). The values every boot writes stay where they were, since the glass was tuned on them; modeset.rs now names them truly.

| Block | Where (dqe0 G#194D_0000 unless named) | Entries | Precision | Bypass |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| ATC, adaptive tone (sunlight) | G#400 | gains, weights, a tone curve from the histogram | | EN, bit 0 of G#400 |
| HSC, hue, saturation and brightness, local and global | G#800 | gains by hue, polynomials | | EN, bit 0 of G#800 |
| gamma matrix | G#1400, coefficients from +4 | 3×3 and 3 offsets | 14-bit coefficients and offsets | EN, bit 0 |
| degamma | G#1800, x from +4, y from +G#48 | 33 programmable (x, y) points, one curve for all three channels (the driver takes red's) | 13-bit x and y, piecewise linear | EN, bit 0 |
| linear matrix | G#1C00, coefficients from +4 | 3×3 and 3 offsets | 16-bit coefficients, 14-bit offsets | EN, bit 0 |
| CGC, a 3D table | dqe0-cgc G#194F_0000: CON +0, R +G#1000, G +G#4000, B +G#7000 | 17×17×17 = A#4913 nodes a channel, two a word (A#2457 words), or by its DMA from DRAM (cgc-dma G#1990_E000) | 13-bit, interpolated between nodes (how: undocumented) | CGC_CON bit 0 |
| CGC dither | G#2800 | one word | as the display dither's | EN, bit 0 |
| regamma 0, 1, 2 | G#2400, G#4C00, G#5000; R, G and B x and y from +4, +G#48, +G#8C, +G#D0, +G#114, +G#158 | 33 programmable (x, y) points a channel | 13-bit x and y, piecewise linear | EN, bit 0 of each |
| display dither | G#2C00 | one word: EN (bit 0), MODE (1: shift, a plain truncation), FRAME_CON (2: temporal), FRAME_OFFSET (3-4), table A or B by channel (5-7) | to the link's 8 bits | EN |

What 1.0 is: the tables as read after the jump are identities whose degamma x runs 0 to G#3E0 by G#20 (a 10-bit input) and y 0 to G#F80 by G#80, and whose regamma x and y both run 0 to G#F80 by G#80: one is G#1000, 12 bits in a 13-bit field. Each table's 33rd entry read back as one step (G#20, G#80), not as the top: unexplained. The two matrices' unity, their sign convention and their clamp are undocumented: unmeasured.

**The order** (inferred from the register order and the order the driver programs the stages; not measured): ATC, HSC, gamma matrix, degamma, linear matrix, CGC, CGC dither, regamma, display dither. The DQE's shadow latches on DECON's SHD_REG_UP_REQ bit 24.

**What Android runs** (the oracle's ON capture, 2026-10-16): the CGC on with its table, 10 bpc in, 8 out, the display dither on. After the jump of 2026-11-12 every stage read off with identity tables and the display dither 0: the DECON reset at the start of the modeset looks to reset the DQE with it (Android's display dither and its LPD word G#1C read back at what look like reset values). After a jump from Android the modeset writes every stage off all the same (modeset.rs `dqe_cold`: the jump is a killswitch, nothing Android left is relied on).

**Could the DQE carry the bake?** The owner (2026-11-12): "So there's a colour table we have access to that saves a click or two?" The mapping is natural:
- degamma: the canvas's working value v (gamma 2) to light l = tanh²(k·v), the decode and the roll-off in one curve, the same for the three channels, which is what one table gives;
- linear matrix: the ODT (E's gains, K);
- regamma: the panel's p^(1/2.19), each channel its own if wanted;
- the display dither down to the link's 8 bits.

A brightness change is then a degamma table (34 words) and a shadow update, with nothing re-baked; the canvas, apps, icons, the wallpaper and the captures stay in working values; the present goes back to a plain 10-bit pack.

What it costs, against today's software path:
- **Shadows.** The light between degamma and regamma sits in 13-bit fields with one at G#1000, so (unless the datapath is wider than its tables) light has steps of 2^-12 of the peak there. The first step above black then lands at 8-bit code 5.7 after the 1/2.19, the next at 7.9 and 9.5: everything between black and code 5.7 is lost and the steps above it are about two codes until code 13 or so, the bottom eight stops the panel can show. The gamma-2 path keeps every code. Whether the datapath is wider than the tables is the first thing to measure.
- **Kinks.** Every table is piecewise linear, C⁰ at its 33 knots (the CGC at its node planes), so each knot is a step in the slope of every gradient crossing it, against calculus or else. With the knots placed geometrically (about three an octave over ten octaves) the curve's error inside a segment is about 1 % of light for the roll-off's quadratic foot and 0.2 % for the 1/2.19; the slope steps at the knots are of the same order.
- **The dither.** Ours (an ordered pattern turned thru the four phases, about 14 bits effective) can no longer be the last step: a nonlinear table after it re-quantizes to 8 bits and keeps none of its pattern. The DQE's display dither becomes the last quantizer: a fixed ordered table (A or B by channel) over the bits below the 8 (four of them, if the datapath is 12 bits), with a two-bit frame offset at the panel's frame rate under FRAME_CON, not our phases. Ours could still take the 16-bit canvas to the 10-bit layer, two dithers stacked at different depths, which the glass disliked on 2026-10-22.
- **Unknown numbers.** The matrices' unity, sign and clamp, and the CGC's interpolation: measured, not assumed, before any of it carries a picture.
- **The jump.** Tables in the DQE are state Android writes too: every jump rewrites them all (the modeset already resets the block).

What it would buy: the per-pixel work stays one pack (the bake already made the present about one instruction a pixel), and the bake's work per colour and per brightness goes away (the palette, the icons' mips, the wallpaper's re-bake, op 65 and `EV_BAKE` for the apps, the unbake for captures). The gain is simplicity and a free brightness change; the price is the shadows and the dither, both unmeasured.

**Better placed, unread: the layer's HDR block.** Each layer's DPP has one (layer 0's `hdr0` at G#1998_0000; cal_9865 regs-hdr.h): an EOTF table of A#40 points with 10-bit x and 16-bit y, a 3×3 gamut matrix (S2.16 coefficients, 14-bit offsets), a tone-mapping stage, and an OETF of A#48 points with 14-bit x and 10-bit y. Its light carries 16 bits between the tables, four more than the DQE's, which removes most of the shadow cost, and it is per layer (a window could keep its own curve). Android's dump reads it all zero (off). ferros has never read it, and whether its words are secure is unknown: nothing is read there until a cold boot's first read says it is plain (the poison rule, §8).

**To measure before deciding** (the owner's go, on a cold boot, the i1 Pro on a patch): the DQE's depth (degamma and regamma as exact inverses, v² and √, and the darkest 32 codes of a 10-bit grey ramp read against the same ramp with the stages off); the matrices' unity and sign (one coefficient at a time on a grey); the same two for the HDR block once its words are read.

## 8. Power-up from off (2026-11-03, the owner's decision 4 carried out)

Why now: a jump from Android often finds the DPU powered down (DECON hibernation: Android switches the DPU's domains off between frames whenever the screen is still; the panel keeps its image), and sometimes the panel off too (screen timed out). ferros has only ever adopted a running pipe (ABL's or Android's), so those jumps came up without a picture, and touching the dead DPU poisoned EL3 (the poison rule, display.rs). Both cases are needed for the cold path anyway: ferros turning its own screen fully off and on, and a boot without ABL's splash.

Sources: the stock driver (display-gs `cal_9865`, `exynos_drm_{decon,dsim,hibernation}.c`), the CAL engine (`flexpmu_cal_local_zuma.h`, `pmucal_local.c`, `exynos-pd_el3.c`), the panel (DISPLAY-HK3.md), DISPLAY-MODESET.md, and the oracle (`oracle/display/husky-hk3/ON`: the complete power-on's DSI packets and DPU registers).

The two starting states:
- **A, hibernation:** DPU domains off, DSIM and its PHY off, the panel on in command mode holding its frame. Android's exit (`exynos_hibernation_exit`): domains on, DECON `decon_reg_init`, DSIM `dsim_reg_init` + ULPS exit + start; the panel is not touched; the next frame's commit configures the DPP and starts DECON.
- **B, panel off:** as A, then the panel's power-up: rails, reset, the init set, display-on.

The layers, in order, and who owns each:
1. **Domains (kernel, EL3 only):** DPUB first (DPUF0 and DPUF1 are its children). Each: CONFIGURATION (G#1546_1D00 / 1D80 / 1E00) bit 0 = 1 through SMC_CMD_PRIV_REG; poll STATUS (+4) bit 0, 5 ms; then the D_TZPC restore SMC G#8200_0410 (1, need_smc, 2) once for that power-on (need_smc G#1941_0204 / G#1981_0204 / G#19C1_0204; a second prepare on an already prepared domain never returns, camera.rs); then the CMU restore (CMU_DPUB G#1940_0000: +G#1800 divider, +G#600 / +G#610 user muxes, QCH +G#3060-30F0, +G#800 CONTROLLER_OPTION; CMU_DPUF0/1 G#1980_0000 / G#19C0_0000: +G#600, QCH +G#3044-30BC, +G#800) and the DPUF sysregs (G#1982_0000 / G#19C2_0000 +G#104, +G#108). No source holds these values (Linux saves them at power-off), so they are **measured live on a cold boot**, where ABL leaves the DPU running, and restored from that table.
2. **DPHY isolation off (kernel, EL3):** PMU G#3EB8 bit 0 through the PMU's atomic alias (PRIV_REG write of bit index 0 to G#1546_FEB8).
3. **SysMMUs** (G#1984_0000 / G#19C4_0000): translation stays off, as ABL leaves it (ferros's framebuffers are physical); checked after power-on, not assumed.
4. **DECON from reset (display server):** `decon_reg_init` (DISPLAY-MODESET.md: CLOCK_CON, the output SRAMs, command mode, background size, the urgent/DTA set, TRIG_CON, data path, DSC encoders and PPS), values from the oracle's ON dump.
5. **DSIM from reset (display server):** `dsim_reg_init` in its order (sysreg PHY reset, OSC clock, SWRST, lanes, escape clocks, the PLL at ABL's 1368 Mbps PMSK, lock, lane enable, PHY reset release, word clock, config), then in A the ULPS exit, then start.
6. **Panel (B only; kernel for power, display server for commands):** VDDI is always on; VCI (LDO14M) on + 10 ms; reset gpa6-0 high 1 / low 1 / high 5 ms; VDDD (LDO22M) on; `9D 01`, the PPS, the init set (`11` + 10 ms … + 110 ms, `35`, …), `C3`/`F2`, one TE, the display-on set and `29`. Which state the panel is in is read, not guessed: VCI's enable bit through ACPM.
7. **Start (display server):** the TE pin (gpp0-3) to function 2 (GPP0_CON bits 12-15; ABL leaves G#0000_2010 on a cold boot, and a jump from a screen-off Android finds it an input: corrected 2026-11-04 from G#F, which is not the TE function), DECON start, the first frame; then the existing 10 bpc modeset and present path as on every boot.

The DPU check everywhere (hv at the jump, kernel fact 30, kernel display.rs) reads CONFIGURATION and STATUS both, because the request alone reads on while a domain is still powering up.

Tested without Android: `diag display cycle hib` and `diag display cycle off` on a cold boot take ferros's own display down into state A or B (DECON stop, DSIM ULPS or stop, panel off and rails off for B, domains off) and back up through this path, repeatable without anyone's hands; then the jump.

Status (2026-11-03 evening): layers 1-2 built and verified (dpu_power.rs, `diag dpu [on|off]`): three off/on cycles in a row, every restored CMU word back at the cold boot's, nothing hung. What the bisection taught: (a) the D_TZPC save (G#8200_0410 with 0) must precede every power-off, or the restore after it never returns; (b) the user muxes must be on their clocks before any DPU register is touched (a bare power-on leaves them 0, and a DECON access hung the core); (c) CONTROLLER_OPTION last, after all three domains; (d) **secure registers**: the DPUF sysregs and the zero slots of the Q-channel ranges read 0 non-secure because D_TZPC makes them secure, and touching one poisons EL3 like a dead domain; only words the cold boot read as non-zero are read or written; (e) the DSIM is in reset after a power-up (SYSREG_DPUB +G#1008 M_RESETN 0) and an access then hangs: its init sets that first. Layers 4, 5 and 7 built and verified on the bench (2026-11-03): with the DPU switched off by `diag dpu off`, a restarted display server reads SYSTEM fact 30 = 1, powers the DPU through its `dpu` switch (grant 101) and runs `modeset::from_off`: the forced modeset with its snapshots from the captured running pipe (oracle/display/husky-hk3/cold-pipe), no DSIM stop, the stock ULPS exit before the link's start; the pipe from reset in 14 ms, DSIM alive at 1368 Mbps, frames completing. The PMU's power state is read plainly (EL3's PRIV_REG read of STATUS does not return it). Next: the jump (Android's hibernation, the real ULPS case).

State B built and verified on the bench (2026-11-03 night, owner asleep, so verified by the panel's own answers, not by eye): the kernel's `panel` switch (panel_power.rs, grant 102) turns VCI (L14M_CTRL G#38 bit 7) on, 10 ms, then the reset pulse on gpa6-0 (high 1, low 1, high 5 ms; pin 0's bits alone, the bank holding the killswitch's volume-up key), and `panel-vddd` (grant 103) VDDD (L22M_CTRL G#40) after the init set; only enable bits are written, the voltages stay ABL's. SYS_FACTS 81 is VCI's state (read once at the service loop's start, then as the switch sets it). A display server that finds 81 = 1 powers the rails, then inside the modeset after the link's start sends DSC on and the PPS, the init set, VDDD, the power-on feature block, `53 20` and the E4/F4 block (ferros_dsi::hk3 POWER_ON_INIT, _FEATURES, _TAIL, transcribed from the oracle's ON capture with its waits: 10 ms after sleep out, 110 ms before TE on), and display on from the panel thread after the first frame start. The bench: display op 31 (`panel off`: display off, sleep in, 100 ms, the switch off; `ferros-bridge spawn capture.bin 1F --use display`), then `diag "restart display"`. Twice in a row: the init 25 packets, the features 50, the tail 8, nothing stuck, 128 ms from rails on to the end of the tail; the panel then answered get_power_mode G#9D (booster, sleep out, normal mode, display on) after its first frame, and frames ran on at the rest rate.
